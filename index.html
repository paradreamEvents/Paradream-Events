    Frequently Asked Questions

    What services does Paradream Events offer?

    Paradream Events is an event planning and entertainment company in Lebanon. We offer full event planning and coordination, venue sourcing, décor and table setups, live show parades, oriental zaffah, live music, photo booths, characters and LED mascots, fire, LED and circus shows, inflatable games, catering, bar and chocolate stations, and digital or printed invitation cards.

    Which occasions do you cover, and where in Lebanon do you work?

    We plan proposals, engagements, bachelor parties, weddings, baptisms, first communions, gender reveals, birthdays, Christmas events and corporate events. We are based in Furn El Chebbak, Beirut, and work across Lebanon. Events outside Beirut include a travel fee, which our online quote calculator estimates.

    How much does a zaffah or live parade cost?

    Zaffah and live show parades are priced per musician or performer, starting from $65 each, so the total depends on the size of your group. Catering is priced per guest. Use the quote calculator on this page for an instant estimate, then send us your details for a confirmed quote.

    How do I book Paradream for my event?

    Pick your occasion on the Occasions page and fill in its short form, or use the Contact page. Our team reviews every request and replies within 24 to 48 hours. If your date is close, call or message us on WhatsApp at +961 81 406 046.

    Can you help me find a venue?

    Yes. Tell us whether you want an indoor or outdoor space, a panoramic mountain view, a beach sunset or a private venue, along with your guest count and budget, and we will help you find and secure the right place.

    Do you offer digital or printed invitation cards?

    Yes. We design digital invitations ready to share on WhatsApp or email, printed cards, plexi and board cards, thank-you cards and return gifts, all matched to your celebration. You can see how an invitation opens on the Our Services page.

    What is the overall cost of organizing the event, and what factors influence the final price?

    The overall cost of organizing an event depends on various factors, including the venue, catering, entertainment, staffing, and any additional services or equipment required. The final price is influenced by the size and scope of the event, the complexity of logistics, and the level of customization desired. We provide a detailed estimate based on your specific requirements to ensure transparency and help you manage your budget effectively.

    How far in advance should I start planning my event?

    It is recommended to start planning your event at least 6 to 12 months in advance. This allows enough time for securing venues, arranging vendors, and addressing any logistical details, ensuring a well-organized and successful event.
    For larger or more complex events, additional planning time may be necessary.

    What factors should I consider when setting the event budget?

    When setting the event budget, the factors to consider will vary based on the type and scale of the event.
    But the main factors can be (the venue cost, catering and beverages, entertainment and live shows, theme, table decoration, chocolate, staffing…etc)

    Why should I consider hiring an event planner for my upcoming event?

    At the end, engaging a professional event organizer during this period is essential, as their expertise ensures meticulous planning, effective problem-solving, Strategic Planning, Budget Management, Attention to Detail, Access to Resources, Stress Reduction and creative solutions.
